# Partnership Engine

Goal: create high-value revenue without depending on paid ads.

## Target Segments

- Preschools and Montessori chains.
- Homeschool co-ops.
- Pediatric clinics and child-development centers.
- Parenting coaches and therapists.
- Employer benefit brokers.
- Kids activity brands and toy companies.
- International education distributors.

## Offer Structure

- `$2,500` pilot for 50-100 families for 60 days.
- `$10,000` school/clinic annual package for 250-500 families.
- `$25,000-$50,000` chain/employer package.
- 30%-50% first-year revenue share for coaches and creators.

## Partner Pitch

NeuroSpark gives your families a daily home-practice layer: parent-led activities, printable guidance, progress snapshots, and safe AI-age readiness support. You get a low-lift enrichment benefit and parents get something they can actually do at home.

## Partner Email

Would a 20-minute pilot discussion be useful?

## Weekly Outbound Target

- 100 partner leads researched.
- 50 personalized emails/DMs sent.
- 10 follow-ups sent daily.
- 5 demo calls booked weekly.
- 1 signed pilot every 1-2 weeks once traction begins.

## Demo Agenda

1. Partner problem.
2. Parent home-practice gap.
3. NeuroSpark product demo.
4. Printable guide and report demo.
5. Data/privacy boundaries.
6. Pilot structure and pricing.
7. Next step and decision date.

## Completion Criteria

Mark complete when:

- 3 paid pilots are signed, or
- one `$25k+` annual partner contract closes.
