# Revenue Sprint Playbook

Goal: first `$10k`, then `$50k`, through founder annual, Family Pro, webinars, referrals, and creators.

## Funnel

1. Short video or community post.
2. Free 7-Day AI-Age Starter Pack.
3. Email sequence with daily activity.
4. Live webinar or app demo.
5. Founder Annual or Family Pro offer.
6. Referral reward: one month or printable bundle.

## 30-Day AI-Age Readiness Challenge

Offer:

- Daily NeuroSpark activity.
- Printable weekly guide.
- Weekly parent workshop.
- Progress/report check-in.
- Family Pro upsell.

Pricing:

- Founder Annual: `$79` or local equivalent.
- Family Pro: `$199-$299`.
- Partner bundle: `$2,500+` pilot.

## Webinar Script

Title: AI-age readiness without more screen time.

Structure:

1. The parenting problem: kids need judgment, focus, creativity, and agency.
2. Why more apps/screens are not the answer.
3. Show one NeuroSpark activity.
4. Show printable parent guide.
5. Show Brain Map/report.
6. Explain founder offer.
7. Invite questions.
8. Ask for purchase or referral.
