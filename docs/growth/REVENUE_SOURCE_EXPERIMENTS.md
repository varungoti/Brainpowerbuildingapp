# Revenue Source Experiments

Test at least 10 revenue sources beyond basic subscriptions. Keep only those with fast cash or strong retention signal.

## Experiment Format

- Name:
- Segment:
- Offer:
- Price:
- Channel:
- Hypothesis:
- Success metric:
- Fail metric:
- Duration:
- Decision: scale, iterate, or kill.

## Initial 12 Experiments

1. Founder Family Annual
   - Price: `$49-$79`.
   - Success: 2%+ purchase rate from soft-launch users.

2. Family Pro Annual
   - Price: `$199-$299`.
   - Success: 10+ purchases or strong founder-call demand.

3. 30-Day AI-Age Challenge
   - Price: annual/pro bundle.
   - Success: webinar-to-purchase conversion above 5%.

4. Printable Bundle Store
   - Price: `$9-$19` bundle, `$49` seasonal bundle.
   - Success: purchases from non-subscribers.

5. Creator Affiliate
   - Commission: 30%-50% first payment.
   - Success: 5 creators publish and 1+ converts.

6. Preschool Pilot
   - Price: `$2,500`.
   - Success: one paid pilot or serious procurement conversation.

7. Clinic Snapshot Pilot
   - Price: referral/pilot bundle.
   - Success: clinic agrees to review/share parent snapshot flow.

8. Employer Benefit Pilot
   - Price: `$5,000-$15,000`.
   - Success: 10 HR conversations and one proposal.

9. Parent Workshop
   - Price: `$19-$49` or Pro upsell.
   - Success: 20 paid attendees or Pro conversions.

10. Coach Certification Waitlist
   - Price: `$99-$499` later.
   - Success: 50 qualified coach leads.

11. AI-Age Standard Licensing
   - Price: `$99-$499/month` small partner, `$5k+` annual.
   - Success: partner LOI or pilot payment.

12. Marketplace Affiliate Bundle
   - Price: affiliate commissions.
   - Success: legal-approved disclosure and parent-positive conversion.

## Kill Rules

Kill or pause if:

- It creates compliance risk.
- It needs child data for marketing.
- It produces attention but no leads/revenue.
- CAC cannot plausibly pay back.
- It distracts from a channel with stronger signal.
