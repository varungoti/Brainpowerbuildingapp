# Launch Foundation

This is the canonical launch foundation for NeuroSpark growth execution.

## ICPs

Primary parent ICP:

- Parents of children ages 3-8.
- Already paying for enrichment, Montessori, tutoring, homeschool, STEM, speech/OT support, parenting courses, or printable learning materials.
- Wants practical at-home guidance, not theory.
- Values safety, privacy, trust, and evidence-aligned language.

Secondary buyer ICPs:

- Preschools and Montessori operators.
- Homeschool pods and co-ops.
- Parenting coaches and therapists.
- Pediatric clinics and child-development centers.
- Employer family-benefit teams.
- Parenting creators and educators.

## Positioning

Primary positioning:

> AI-age readiness for kids, guided by parents.

Approved phrases:

- Parent-led developmental activities.
- Executive function.
- Guided play.
- Printable parent guides.
- Safe AI support.
- Screen-light routines.
- Pediatrician-shareable observations, not diagnosis.

Avoid:

- Medical or diagnostic claims.
- Guaranteed developmental outcomes.
- Brain-training transfer claims.
- Fear-based AI messaging.
- Outreach to children.

## Launch Offers

Consumer:

- Free: limited daily pack, brain map preview, printable sample, science/trust guide.
- Family Premium: `$9.99/month` or `$79/year`.
- India annual launch: `₹4,999-₹7,999/year`.
- Family Pro: `$199-$499/year`.

Partners:

- Pilot: `$2,500-$10,000` for 50-100 families, 60 days.
- Annual school/clinic: `$10,000-$50,000`.
- Employer/enterprise: `$25,000-$100,000+`.

## Analytics Checklist

Required events:

- Landing page visit.
- Lead magnet submit.
- Signup.
- Onboarding step views.
- First pack generated.
- First activity opened.
- First activity completed.
- Printable guide created.
- Paywall viewed.
- Plan selected.
- Checkout started.
- Purchase completed.
- Purchase failed.

Existing coverage:

- Product analytics supports paywall, onboarding, first-activity, attribution, and PostHog forwarding.
- Growth Command Center tracks leads, OKRs, revenue checkpoints, readiness, and approval queue.

## Completion Criteria

Mark launch foundation complete when:

- Pricing is aligned across app and marketing site.
- Lead magnet captures leads.
- Analytics events are wired or documented.
- Growth Command Center can track daily OKRs and revenue.
- Production gates and kill switches are reviewed.
