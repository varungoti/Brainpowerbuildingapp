# Pre-Launch Audience Playbook

Goal: collect qualified demand before public launch.

## Daily Operating Rhythm

Every weekday:

- Publish 3 short videos.
- Publish 1 carousel or printable preview.
- Comment thoughtfully on 30 relevant parent/education posts.
- Send 20 creator/educator/partner outreach messages.
- Post in 3 communities with useful examples, not spam.
- Capture objections and questions in Growth Command Center.
